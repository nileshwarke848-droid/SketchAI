A free, private drawing studio

Turn a quick sketch into a creative idea with SketchAI

SketchAI is a browser-based AI drawing app for turning rough doodles into editable compositions. Draw an object on the canvas and the built-in classifier suggests what it might be; choose a suggestion to add clean artwork to your scene. No sign-up, API key or drawing upload is required.

How SketchAI works

  1. Draw a simple object with the pencil tool using a mouse, trackpad, stylus or touch screen.
  2. Pause briefly while the browser crops and normalizes the sketch for its local neural-network classifier.
  3. Review a ranked list of possible objects and confidence estimates. AI guesses are suggestions, not guaranteed identifications.
  4. Choose an illustration to add it as an editable object. Move, resize, rotate, recolor, duplicate, reorder or delete it independently.

Drawing and editing tools

The canvas includes pencil, text, fill, selection, classic eraser and brush-style doodle eraser tools. Separate sketch, fill and object layers make it possible to edit accepted illustrations without flattening the whole scene. Zoom and pan the canvas while keeping its document coordinates intact.

Privacy and AI recognition

SketchAI performs sketch recognition in the browser using a TensorFlow.js model. The classifier returns likely labels and confidence estimates; it can misunderstand abstract, unusual or multiple-object sketches. Sketches are not sent to a SketchAI server because no application backend is required.

Frequently asked questions

What is SketchAI?

SketchAI is a free, browser-based drawing studio. Sketch an object, see ranked AI guesses, then add a clean illustration to your canvas as an editable object.

Does SketchAI upload or store my drawings?

No. Sketch recognition runs locally in your browser. SketchAI does not require an account, backend, API key or drawing upload. Your drawing stays in your browser.

How does the AI recognize a sketch?

After you pause drawing, SketchAI crops and normalizes the sketch and runs it through a neural-network classifier in your browser. It shows ranked guesses with confidence estimates; recognition can be wrong, especially for unusual or multi-object sketches.

Are SketchAI's suggestions editable?

Yes. Inserted illustrations and text remain separate scene objects that you can select, move, resize, rotate, recolor, duplicate, reorder or delete.
